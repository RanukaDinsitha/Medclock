A simple unique medicine timer and reminder app built using Flutter.

## Medication reminders and timers

Scheduled reminders and medicine timers are separate features. Use the
**MEDICATIONS** tab to manage scheduled reminders, and the **TIMERS** tab after
taking a medicine to start a countdown for when it can be taken again. Active
timers and medication details are stored locally with Isar. A timer disappears
when its countdown ends; expired timers are also cleared when the app starts.

The countdown is a personal tracking aid, not medical advice. Follow the dose
interval provided by your prescription or healthcare professional.

> Isar 3.x does not support web, so local Isar persistence currently requires a
> native app target.
